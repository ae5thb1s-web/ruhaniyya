What is Ruhaniyya?

Ruhaniyya, the path of spiritual Islam: the path of the Prophet ﷺ, the Seal of the Prophets (Khātam an-Nabiyyīn, خَاتَمُ النَّبِيِّين).

Living by the Sunnah, especially in these times.

Ruhaniyya (روحانية, spirituality) accompanies you on the path of the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ. We give you knowledge and the important sources, remind you of the Sunnah days and offer the material things that strengthen us physically: clothing, accessories and practical helpers for daily life. For Satan tries to make people forget what matters in daily life (“Satan has overcome them and made them forget the remembrance of Allah”, Qur'an 58:19). Ruhaniyya wants to remind you.

وَذَكِّرْ فَإِنَّ الذِّكْرَىٰ تَنفَعُ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ “And remind, for the reminder benefits the believers.” (Qur'an 51:55)
Our goal

To unite the Ummah of the Prophet ﷺ, to strengthen the community and to make the world a better place in the name of Allah, the Almighty.

وَاعْتَصِمُوا بِحَبْلِ اللَّهِ جَمِيعًا وَلَا تَفَرَّقُوا“And hold firmly, all of you, to the rope of Allah and do not be divided.” (Qur'an 3:103)

                                Which zodiac sign the Sun, Moon and planets are in right now, calculated to the arc minute. Plus your personal birth chart.

                                Calculating planet positions …
                                PlanetSignDegreeHouseConstellation

                                ℞ = retrograde (apparently moving backwards, as seen from Earth). House by Placidus for the chosen place. Constellation = the constellation the planet actually stands in front of in the sky (IAU boundaries).

                                Void-of-course Moon

                                In astrological tradition, the time in which the Moon forms no more major aspect to any planet before its next sign change. Traditionally one starts nothing important then.

                                  Retrograde planets ℞

                                  Retrograde means: seen from Earth, the planet appears to move backwards through the zodiac for a while, because Earth overtakes it on its orbit or is overtaken by it. The shadow phase is the stretch before and after, in which the planet passes through the same degrees.

                                      During a solar or lunar eclipse, the eclipse prayer (Ṣalāt al-Kusūf or al-Khusūf) is Sunnah: “The sun and the moon are two signs of Allah. They are not eclipsed because of anyone’s death or life. When you see this, pray and call upon Allah.” (al-Bukhari 1043)
