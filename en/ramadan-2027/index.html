Ramadan 2027: when does it start?

First day of fasting (Ramadan 1448)Monday, 8 February 2027Diyanet / Umm al-Qura · by moon sighting often one day later: Tuesday, 9 February 2027

Ramadan 1448 AH falls in February and March 2027. Diyanet (Turkey, DITIB) and the Saudi Umm al-Qura calendar announce their dates in advance, while communities that wait for the sighting of the crescent often start one day later.

Frequently asked questions

When does Ramadan 2027 start?

According to Diyanet and Umm al-Qura the first day of fasting is Monday, 8 February 2027. Communities following moon sighting often start on Tuesday, 9 February 2027.

When is Eid al-Fitr 2027?

Eid al-Fitr is expected on Tuesday, 9 March 2027 (Diyanet/Umm al-Qura) or Wednesday, 10 March 2027 by moon sighting.
