The White Days are the three days around the full moon, the 13th, 14th and 15th of the lunar month. The Prophet ﷺ recommended fasting on them: “If you fast three days of the month, fast the 13th, 14th and 15th.” (at-Tirmidhi 761, an-Nasa'i 2424). Whoever fasts three days every month is as if he fasted the whole year (al-Bukhari 1979).
